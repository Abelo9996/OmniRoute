## TL;DR — nawa ne na inferens kyauta OmniRoute ke tarawa a zahiri?

Waɗannan alkaluma suna bayyana asusun ɓangarori na uku daban-daban da sharuɗɗan cancanta a faɗin kundin bayanan. OmniRoute ba ya ba ku wannan kasafin: dole ne ku samu asusu ko bayanan shaida da ake buƙata, ku haɗa masu samarwa da suka cancanta, kuma ku mutunta iyakokinsu. Ainihin ƙarfin da za a iya amfani da shi na iya zama ƙasa, har ma da sifili a sabon shigarwa da ba shi da wata hanya da ta cancanta. Masu samarwa da aka yi wa alamar `tos: avoid` ba sa shiga turawa ta atomatik ta tsohuwa.

**An duba 2026-10-08:** [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/pricing) yana bayar da kiredit na talla na $5 sau ɗaya wanda ke buƙatar hanyar biyan kuɗi, kuma zai ƙare bayan kwanaki 30. ## Sabuntawar 2026-06-17 — abin da ya canza tun daga 2026-06-05

Binciken yanar gizo da wakilai 50 suka gudanar (takardun hukuma + labaran kwanaki 7 da suka gabata, tare da tabbatarwa ta hanyar gwajin adawa) ya sabunta dukan kundin. Muhimman bayanai:

- **An cire / babu matakin kyauta (2026):** `chutes` (matakin kyauta ya ƙare 2026-03), `phind` (kamfanin ya rufe 2026-01), `kluster` (an daina shi 2026-06-09 → MITO), `gitlawb` + `gitlawb-gmi` (an soke kyautar MiMo 2026-05-24, tallan Nemotron ya ƙare 2026-06 — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `aimlapi` (an dakatar da matakin kyauta — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `yi` (an janye Yi-Light, yanzu biya gwargwadon amfani — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `featherless-ai` (babu matakin kyauta a halin yanzu), `chipotle` ("Pepper AI" na Chipotle — widget ɗin taɗi na Amelia da aka gano yadda yake aiki, wato `amelia.chipotle.com`, wanda mai samarwar yake sadarwa da shi yanzu yana mayar da 404 a kowace hanya, har da tushen shafin; an dakatar/an matsar da backend ɗin, an tabbatar 2026-09-15 — an cire shi gaba ɗaya daga kundin, #13131/#4037). `iflytek` / `sparkdesk` suna nan a jeri amma suna ɗauke da bayanin taka-tsantsan game da ToS (Spark Lite kyauta ne; ToS yana taƙaita amfani da proxy/relay).
- **Gemini** — an rufe `2.0 Flash` / `2.0 Flash-Lite` a 2026-06-01 kuma `2.5 Pro` ya fita daga matakin kyauta (2026-04); yanzu matakin kyauta ya taƙaita ga **iyalan Flash kawai** (2.5/3/3.1/3.5 Flash + Gemma). Yanzu kundin yana **haɗa** iyalan Flash a tafki guda (a baya adadin ya ƙaru fiye da kima saboda an ƙirga kowane nau'i dabam: 462M → 60M).
- **Lambobin da aka gyara:** `cloudflare-ai` 122M → **30M** (ainihin 10k-Neurons/rana), an sake rarraba `doubao` a matsayin kuɗin rajista na sau ɗaya (ba mai maimaituwa ba), `llm7` 4M → **150M** (kamar yadda aka rubuta, tokens 5M/rana), an dakatar da endpoints na "-Free" na `together` → an kuma cire kuɗin rajista na $25 (ana buƙatar mafi ƙarancin saye na $5, babu gwajin kyauta), Lokacin Preview na `longcat` ya ƙare + an janye samfurorin Flash → **LongCat-2.0** kawai, an sake rarraba shi a matsayin kuɗin rajista na sau ɗaya na tokens **10M** (yana buƙatar KYC, ba mai maimaituwa ba).
- **Sabbin masu samar da sabis na kyauta da aka gano:** ⭐ **Kilo Code** (`kilo-gateway` — jerin "Auto Free" mai sauyawa: iyalan NVIDIA Nemotron 3, StepFun, Poolside, Nex-N2-Pro), ⭐ **OpenCode Zen** (`opencode-zen` — samfuran coding na kyauta 6 masu sauyawa), ⭐ **Z.AI / Zhipu** (`glm-cn` — GLM-4-Flash / 4.5-Flash / 4.7-Flash kyauta na dindindin + kyautar rajista 20M), da `arcee-ai` Trinity Large Preview.
- **Sabbin matakai na gaskiya** (duba Hanyar Aiki): wani rukuni na _kyauta-na-dindindin-amma-ba-shingen-iyaka_ (ainihin damar amfani mai maimaituwa, babu iyakar token da za a ƙirga) da kuma _ƙarin damar amfani bayan ajiya_ (OpenRouter $10 → +24M/wata), an nuna dukansu **dabam-dabam** don kada su taɓa ƙara babban adadin fiye da gaskiya.

> Cikakken teburin kowane mai samar da sabis da ke ƙasa shi ne **hoton bayanai na 2026-06-05**; sauye-sauyen da ke sama sun maye gurbinsa. Tushen kai-tsaye kuma tabbatacce shi ne kundin kowane samfur `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.ts`.

## Tsaruka biyu — ƙirga da yanke shawara

OmniRoute yana amsa "shin kyauta ne?" ta hanyar tsaruka biyu waɗanda da gangan suke karanta
mabambantan tushe:

| Tsari              | Tushen gaskiya                                                                                                                                                                                    | Wuraren amfani                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Ƙirga / nunawa** | Kundin da aka warware — tushen da aka tura wanda aka ɗora masa bayanan Radar (`getRadarCatalog`)                                                                                                  | Jimillar matakin kyauta, katin kasafin kuɗi, allunan bayanai                                                                   |
| **Yanke shawara**  | Kundin da aka tura kawai (`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS` a cikin `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.data.ts`) tare da ƙa'idojin hasashe na gida (ƙarin suna na `:free`, farashin sifili, `grantsFreeAccess`) | Duk masu amfani da `src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`: shigo da samfuri, turawar `auto/*`, `GET /v1/models`, da samfotin burauza |

Ƙirga na iya inganta a duk lokacin da ake samun feed. Yanke shawara yana ci gaba da dogaro da
abin da aka fitar, don haka amsar za ta kasance iri ɗaya a burauza da sabar,
ana iya maimaita ta ba tare da intanet ba, kuma ana iya gwada ta ba tare da rumbun bayanai ba. Barin samfotin
burauza ya karanta tushe ɗaya yayin da shigo da bayanan sabar yake karanta wani zai haifar da
samfotin da bai yi daidai da abin da ke faruwa bayan dannawa ba — ana kiyaye wannan rabuwar
da gangan.

## Hanya & abubuwan lura

- Lambobin **ƙiyasin iyaka mafi girma** ne daga iyakokin matakin kyauta da kowane mai samarwa ya rubuta har zuwa **2026-06-17**, waɗanda aka tattara ta hanyar binciken yanar gizo. Matakan kyauta suna canzawa koyaushe — sake tabbatarwa kafin dogaro da wani adadi.
- **Abin da kowace shigarwa take tabbatarwa a zahiri.** Babu shigarwar da ke ɗauke da ƙimar amincewa ta kowane layi, kuma API ba ya bayar da ita — ɗauki kowane adadin da ke sama a matsayin ƙiyasi mai inganci iri ɗaya da ba a bayyana ba. Bayanai biyu sun bambanta, saboda an tsara su da hannu maimakon a samo su ta hanyar hasashe: shigarwa 44 suna da tabbatacciyar iyakar tsayawa da aka rubuta daban (39 daga cikinsu layukan xKiro ne, waɗanda duk suke da adadin izinin yau da kullum iri ɗaya), kuma shigarwa 13 suna da bayanin amfani da prompts wajen horo. Ana saita `hardStopGuaranteed` ne kawai idan sharuɗɗan mai samarwar da kansa suka ce wuce adadin kyautar zai sa a ƙi buƙatar maimakon a fara cajinka a ɓoye, tare da tushen a cikin sharhi kusa da shigarwar; ba a taɓa saita shi zuwa `true` ta tsohuwa ba, kuma shigarwar da babu wanda ya tabbatar da ita tana kasancewa ba a saita ta ba. Saboda haka rashin tutar tsayawa kai tsaye yana nufin "ba a tabbatar ba", ba "an san za a cajeka" ba. Yanayin **STRICT** (kariyar turawa ta `freeAccessPolicy=strict` da ake kunna ta zaɓi) yana amincewa ne kawai da shigarwar da ke ɗauke da tutar; ana cire duk wani matakin kyauta daban a matsayin `no-hard-stop` (duba `open-sse/services/autoCombo/strictZeroCostFilter.ts`). Lokacin da kariyar ta tace tarin zaɓuɓɓuka, log ɗin da mai gudanarwa zai iya gani yana bayar da rahoton adadin da aka cire da kuma rabon `no-hard-stop` dinsu.
- `estMonthlyFreeTokens` = tokens na wata-wata masu maimaituwa kawai. **Kyaututtukan rajista na lokaci guda ba sa maimaituwa** kuma ana ƙirga su a matsayin 0. Matakan da aka dakatar su ma 0 ne.
- Iyakar token ta yau da kullum → `monthly = daily × 30`. Idan RPD kawai aka rubuta → `RPD × ~800 output tokens × 30`. Idan RPM/TPM kawai ne (babu iyakar yau da kullum) → **babu iyaka** (duba ƙasa).
- **Kyauta ta dindindin, amma babu iyakar token da aka wallafa** (`siliconflow`, `glm-cn`, `tencent`, `baidu`, `kilo-gateway`, `opencode-zen`, `gemini`, `ollama-cloud`): waɗannan damar kyauta ne na gaske masu maimaituwa, waɗanda ƙimar amfani/yawan ayyukan lokaci guda ke iyakancewa. Muna rarraba su a matsayin `recurring-uncapped` kuma **ba ma taɓa tara su** — ninka `RPM × 24/7 × 30d` zai samar da iyakar ƙirƙirarre (irin kumburin da muke ƙi). An jera su ne domin ku san cewa suna nan.
- **Ƙarin da ajiya ke buɗewa:** ƙaramin ƙarin kuɗi na lokaci guda wanda ke ɗaga adadin kyauta har abada (OpenRouter: $10 → buƙatu 1000/rana ≈ +24M/wata). Ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin adadi daban, kuma ba a saka shi cikin babban adadin dindindin.
- **Adadin da cancanta ke kayyadewa** (`eligibilityGate: "regional-identity"`): ainihin adadi ne mai maimaituwa wanda yake buɗewa ne kawai bayan tantance shaida mai alaƙa da yanki (tabbatar da suna na ainihi na babban yankin China a halin yanzu). Ana ƙirga shi bisa ƙa'idar cire maimaituwar tarin iri ɗaya zuwa wani adadi daban (`gatedRecurringTokens`), ba a taɓa saka shi cikin babban adadin dindindin ba. Tsarin (`freeType`) bai canza ba, don haka turawa ma ba ta canza ba.
- **Rukunin hujjoji.** Ƙa'idar ita ce: lamba a cikin kundin tana ambaton tushenta a cikin sharhin `// evidence:` kusa da shigarwar — `public-page` (shafin mai samarwa da kowa zai iya karantawa), `api-public` (endpoint na mai samarwa wanda ba ya buƙatar tantancewa, misali endpoint na tsare-tsaren jama'a na NaraRouter a router.bynara.id), ko `console-verified <date> por <who>` (ana iya ganin adadin ne kawai a cikin console na asusu; sharhin yana rubuta wanda ya gani da lokacin da ya gani, da kuma shafin jama'a da ke cewa akwai iyakar). Halin yanzu: tubalan guda biyar da aka sake bincikawa a 2026-09-02 suna ɗauke da shi (`gemini`, `groq`, `mistral`, `ollama-cloud`, `nara`); shigarwar da suka gabaci sake binciken 2026-09-02 suna gādon binciken da ya gabata har sai an taɓa su; duk wata lamba **sabuwa ko da aka canza** ba tare da sharhin hujja ba kuskure ce. A yau `mistral` kaɗai aka tabbatar ta console.

## Me ya sa adadinmu ya fi na sauran masu tarawa ƙanƙanta

Yawancin alkaluman "free tokens per month" a wannan fanni jimillar alamomin kowane model ne. Namu ba haka yake ba, da gangan:

- **Ana ƙirga kowane shared pool sau ɗaya.** Tsarin kyauta na Mistral yana da izinin 1B/month guda ɗaya ga kowace ƙungiya; jera shi a ƙarƙashin model guda biyar ba ya mayar da shi 5B. Idan aka tara bisa ga kowane model, catalog ɗinmu zai nuna **~7.4B** (an sake lissafawa a 2026-09-03; wannan adadin ba ya ƙarƙashin kariyar CI — a sake auna shi duk lokacin da catalog ya canza) — babban taken yana cewa **~1.47B** domin wannan shi ne abin da account guda ɗaya na kowane provider zai iya kashewa a zahiri.
- **Ana mayar da iyakokin yau da kullum, amma ba rates ba.** Iyakacin tokens/day da aka rubuta yana zama `× 30`; iyakacin requests/day da aka rubuta yana zama `RPD × ~800 tokens × 30`; provider da ke wallafa requests-per-minute kawai ba shi da adadin wata-wata kuma ana jera shi a matsayin _uncapped_, ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin jimilla ba. Ninka rate limit da 24/7 shi ne kumbura adadi da muka ƙi yi.
- **Ana nuna quotas da ke buƙatar tabbatar da shaidar yanki daban** (`+~6M behind regional identity verification`), saboda yawancin masu karatu ba za su iya amfani da su ba.
- **Signup credits na watan farko ne kawai** kuma ana bayar da rahotonsu a matsayin adadi na biyu, ba a taɓa haɗa su da adadin dindindin ba.
- **CI ne ke tabbatar da adadin.** `npm run check:docs-counts` yana sake lissafa jimillolin daga catalog kuma yana hana build kammalawa idan wannan fayil, README ko budget card sun kauce daga gare su.

## Teburin abubuwan ToS da ya kamata a kula da su

> **Alamar ToS shawara ce, ba routing gate ba.** Providers da aka yi wa alamar `tos` har yanzu ana haɗa su cikin routing da combo/fallback ta tsohuwa; alamar tana bayyana ne kawai a `/dashboard/free-tiers` da `/api/free-tier/summary`. Query parameter na `excludeTosAvoid` yana shafar summary view kawai, ba global routing ba. Hukuncin yana cikin `open-sse/config/freeTierCatalog.ts` (na bayani ne, routing engines ba sa karanta shi).

> Taƙaitaccen bayani kan sharuɗɗan kowane provider dangane da self-hosted, single-user personal proxy. `caution` = akwai wani sashe kan personal-use ko proxy da ya kamata a duba; `ambiguous` = ba a bayyana ba; `ok` = an ba da izini a sarari. Bayani ne kawai, ba shawarar doka ba — kai ne za ka yanke hukunci.

## OpenCode Free: ƙuntatawar kwangilar abokin ciniki (#14313)

Mai bayar da sabis na `opencode` marar maɓalli (`https://opencode.ai/zen/v1` na jama'a) yana ƙin duk wata buƙata
da ba ta dace da kwangilar abokin cinikin OpenCode ba tare da **403 `FreeTierError`** da jimlar
_"Za a iya amfani da matakin kyauta na OpenCode ne kawai daga cikin OpenCode"_. Wannan ƙin yarda ne
da ya shafi takamaiman buƙata (hukunci iri ɗaya a kowane asusu don tsarin buƙata iri ɗaya), ba
hana samfurin aiki ko lokacin dakatar da haɗi ba — OmniRoute yana rarraba shi a matsayin `project_route_error`, yana tsallake
kulle samfurin / lokacin dakatarwa, sannan (a kan hanyar `noauth` ta kwaikwayo) yana dakatar da sake zaɓin auto-combo
na ɗan gajeren TTL. Aika buƙatun da ke ɗauke da jerin kayan aiki mara komai ba, `stream: true`, da
taken zaman OpenCode/UA (`opencodeFreeTierContract.ts`), ko kuma ku yi tsammanin 403.
